How to use this panel

  1. Pick your timeframe using the tabs above.
  2. Check active pairs. Only trade instruments showing as full colour for your timeframe.
  3. Mind the clock. All times are UTC. Global blackout is 20:00–23:59 UTC every day.
  4. Open your chart. Apply Perfect Entry Reversal Sniper and follow the signals.
  5. Wait for your trigger. Let the candle with the surfer close, enter on the grey line, TP on green, SL on red.
  6. Risk management. Size from your plan. Accounts under $1,000 should avoid NAS100 and XAUUSD.

Playbook quick notes

Score system (1min & 3min)
Score 4 = 4/4 OOS quarters passed — maximum confidence.

Score 3 = 3/4 OOS quarters passed — high confidence.

Score 2 = 2/4 OOS quarters passed — medium, trade smaller.

Score 1 = 1/4 OOS quarters passed — low, use with caution.
Confidence dots (5min, 15min, 2hr)
Blue = HIGH — window cross-validated cleanly in both halves.

Amber = MEDIUM — both halves positive but windows didn't overlap.

Grey = LOW — one half validated, trade with reduced size.
When to stand down
Blackout window (20:00–23:59 UTC), major red-flag news for your pair, or when the instrument shows as inactive.
Targets & scaling
Use predefined targets from your plan. The blue break-even line is optional — our data assumes you don't use it. Partials are fine.
